Our Story

Placeholder text: North Star Bakery started as a single home oven and a sourdough starter passed down through three generations. What began as weekend baking for neighbors grew into a full storefront where the same starter is still used every single day. We wanted a place where commuters could grab a warm croissant, families could order a birthday cake, and neighbors could stop in just to say hello.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Placeholder text: We mill and source flour from regional farms whenever possible, buy fruit in season from the Saturday farmers market, and use real butter, never shortening. If an ingredient doesn't meet our standard, it doesn't go in the mixing bowl.
